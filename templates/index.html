FOREX LEARNING MODULES

What the AI checks

Market structure / BOS

A BOS is a break beyond a meaningful prior swing in the direction of a possible continuation. Confirm with candle close and context; a wick alone can be a sweep.

Change of character

A CHoCH is an early sign the prior swing sequence may be changing. It is a warning, not proof of a full reversal.

Trendlines

Connect meaningful swing points without forcing the line. More touches can increase relevance, but a break needs context and confirmation.

Liquidity / sweeps

Equal highs/lows and obvious swing points may attract stops. Price can sweep them and reverse, or break and continue.

Zones / order blocks

Mark the origin of a strong displacement and look for a reasoned retest. A zone is an area of interest, not a guaranteed reaction.

Sessions / timeframe

Use higher timeframes for bias and lower timeframes for entries. London/New York activity can increase movement, but volatility is not direction.

RULE-BASED RISK CHECKLIST

Before any entry

1. Identify higher-timeframe direction.
2. Mark recent swing highs/lows and key zones.
3. Wait for structure confirmation, not just a prediction.
4. Define invalidation and stop before entry.
5. Calculate position size from risk budget.
6. Avoid chasing news spikes or trading without a clear setup.
7. Record the reason and review outcomes.
